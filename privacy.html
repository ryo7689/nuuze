Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

In short: Nuuze has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your mixes, settings and sleep/focus records stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what is needed to process and verify subscriptions.

This policy explains how the Nuuze app (“Nuuze”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use it on iPhone, iPad or Android.

1. Information stored on your device

Nuuze stores the following only on your device, using the device’s local app storage:

  • Your sound mixes, saved presets and volume/tone settings
  • Your language preference and other app settings
  • Sleep and focus session records (date, duration, Pomodoro count) and your streak
  • Alarm and timer settings

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the app.

2. Microphone and other permissions

All sounds in Nuuze are generated on your device. Nuuze does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location, and does not record any audio.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions to Nuuze Pro are purchased through Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never receive your name, email address or payment details.

To verify your subscription and unlock Pro features, we use RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • A random, anonymous app user ID generated by the app (not linked to your name or Apple/Google account)
  • Your purchase and subscription history for Nuuze
  • Basic technical information needed to process purchases, such as device type, operating system version, app version, and country or region. Like any internet service, RevenueCat’s servers may also receive your IP address.

This information is used only to provide and restore your subscription. RevenueCat’s handling of this data is described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your information for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not show ads or use analytics or advertising SDKs.

5. Children

Nuuze is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Data retention and your choices

You can delete all data stored on your device at any time by deleting the app. To ask about, or request deletion of, the anonymous purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us at the address below. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act or the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020), you may have the right to access or delete your personal information; we will respond to such requests within 30 days.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
