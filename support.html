Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Nuuze Pro subscription?

Open Nuuze, tap any Pro feature to open the upgrade screen, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Nuuze → Cancel Subscription, or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. You keep Pro access until the end of the current period.

On Android: open the Google Play app → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Nuuze → Cancel.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. For App Store purchases, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play purchases, see Google Play’s refund help page.

Does sound keep playing when my screen is locked?

Yes. Nuuze keeps playing in the background and on the lock screen. If you set a sleep timer, the sound fades out gently when the timer ends.

What is included in Nuuze Pro?

Nuuze Pro unlocks all ambient sounds, unlimited timer length, and more saved mixes. Pricing is shown in the app before you subscribe.

How do I change the language?

Nuuze follows your device language (English, Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese). You can also choose a language manually in the app’s settings.
